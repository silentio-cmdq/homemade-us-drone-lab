# Setup 01 assembly

1. Print frame + canopy. Deburr motor bosses. Test-fit AIO screws without the board first.
2. Install motors. Lead wires toward the center along the molded/printed channels.
3. Soft-mount the AIO if pads exist. Snug, not crushed.
4. Solder or plug motors. Check for whiskers. Strain-relieve.
5. Flash Betaflight. Load the board target, not a random 7" preset.
6. Receiver tab: confirm ELRS packet rate. Failsafe = drop.
7. Motors tab: props off, verify order A=front-left in Betaflight standard, spin direction, then save.
8. Modes: ARM on a 2-position, ANGLE on a switch, BEEPER on a momentary.
9. Props on. Smoke stopper. Hover 3 seconds. Land. Feel motors for grit.
10. Weigh. Write AUW on the tray.

Rates starting point (Angle, whoop):

- RC Rate 0.70 / 0.70 / 0.70
- Super 0.50 / 0.50 / 0.45
- Expo 0.00

Change one number at a time after you can hover without thinking.
