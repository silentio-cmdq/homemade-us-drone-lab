# Setup 03 — 5" FPV, US-leaning stack

**Role:** high-performance freestyle / cinematic quad after the sub-0.5 lb aircraft is boring.

**Regulatory:** this aircraft will weigh well over 250 g. Register it. Mark the number on the exterior. Add Remote ID for outdoor flights. Take Part 107 if the footage is for work.

## US-first shopping list

| Item | First choice | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight controller | Rotor Riot Brave F7 (US, Betaflight `RRIOT_F722`) | ARK FPV if you want PX4 later |
| ESC | Rotor Riot Brave 55A 4-in-1 | ARK 4-in-1, Triumph AM32 |
| Motors | Unusual Machines Brave 2207 1800Kv (5") | KDE or Hoverfly if the frame is heavy |
| Frame | Carbon 5" of your choice + printed TPU corners / cam protection | — |
| RX | ELRS 2.4 diversity | — |
| Video | Document origin. Analog or current lite HD | Thinnest US category |
| GPS (optional) | Compass-equipped module on a mast | Needed for rescue, costs noise and grams |
| Battery | 6S 1100–1300 mAh for freestyle, 6S 1400–1800 for cine | Write cell brand on the card |
| Radio | Same Radiomaster ELRS TX | — |

Confirm current Blue UAS / NDAA listing and Betaflight target notes before you flash.

## Printed parts on this airframe

- TPU camera housing
- TPU arm bumpers
- Antenna mount
- Battery pad
- GoPro mount only if you accepted the mass and registration already

## Do not skip

- Smoke stopper on first power-up.
- Motor direction with props off.
- Failsafe DROP tested by turning the TX off on the bench (props off).
- Blackbox after the first outdoor pack. Tune from logs, not from a YouTuber's 1400 Kv dump.
