# FAA, registration, and shop safety

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. Confirm current text on faa.gov before you fly.

## Two rule sets

**Recreational exception (49 U.S.C. §44809)**  
Flying only for personal enjoyment. You must pass TRUST and follow the recreational rules. Registration is required if takeoff weight is **more than 0.55 lb (250 g)**. Aircraft at or under 250 g flown only recreationally do not need FAA registration. TRUST is still required.

**Part 107**  
Default rule for small UAS under 55 lb that are not flown under the recreational exception (work, business benefit, many "I am just posting this for my company" cases). Remote Pilot Certificate, per-aircraft registration at any weight, Remote ID, airspace authorization as required.

You pick one rule set per flight. You cannot mix them on the same takeoff.

## What "0.5 lb" means here

- 0.50 lb = **226.8 g**
- FAA line = **250 g / 0.55 lb**

Design the starter aircraft for **≤ 220 g AUW** so tape, a longer antenna, or a slightly fatter pack does not surprise you. If a later airframe exceeds 250 g, register it and add Remote ID before outdoor flight.

## Recreational minimums (summary)

- Pass TRUST and carry proof.
- Fly within visual line of sight.
- Give way to manned aircraft.
- Do not interfere with emergency response.
- Stay at or below 400 ft AGL in Class G unless you are at an authorized site / event.
- Check airspace (B4UFLY, LAANC) every flight.
- Register and mark the aircraft if it is over 250 g.
- Registered aircraft generally need Remote ID outdoors unless inside a FRIA.

## Part 107 minimums (summary)

- Remote Pilot Certificate (knowledge test; 16+).
- Register each aircraft ($5, 3 years), mark it, carry proof.
- Remote ID.
- VLOS unless waived, daylight or anti-collision lighting, 100 mph max, 400 ft AGL unless within 400 ft of a structure, etc.
- Waivers exist; they are not a shortcut for a first build.

## Shop and flight safety

- Props off until the radio is bound, failsafe is tested, and motors respond in the correct directions.
- Lipo: balance charger, fire bag, no charging on a couch, storage at ~3.8 V/cell.
- First hover over grass, not over people, dogs, or a glass table.
- Eye protection when spinning props on the bench.
- If a pack is punctured or puffed after a crash, isolate it outdoors on concrete.
- Do not fly under the influence. Do not fly through crowds.

## What this repo will not cover

Payloads or software intended to harm people, disable aircraft, or evade lawful identification. Keep the work in civil photography, mapping, FPV sport, research, and inspection.
